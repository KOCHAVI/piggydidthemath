Privacy Policy

App: "Piggy Did The Math Uploader" · Last updated: November 14, 2026

What this app is

Piggy Did The Math Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @piggydidthemath to publish that channel's own videos and to read that channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google user data it accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos, with titles, descriptions and the AI-content disclosure.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's own video list and status.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own views, watch time and audience retention, used to learn which videos work better.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to improve future videos for the same channel.
  • The sign-in token and analytics numbers are stored only on the channel owner's own computer/server. They are not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
  • No data is collected from viewers or other YouTube users.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens and analytics are deleted from the owner's machine on request or when the tool is no longer used.

Contact

Questions: reach the channel through its YouTube About page.
